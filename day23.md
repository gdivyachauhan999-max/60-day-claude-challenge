## Day 23 – Customer & MVP Blueprint

## Overview

Today, I worked on building a complete Customer & MVP Blueprint for my startup idea, SkillFolio AI. The objective was to deeply understand the target audience, identify their biggest challenges, validate assumptions, and define the most important MVP features before starting product development.

This exercise helped me understand that successful startups are built by solving real customer problems rather than simply building features.

---

## Startup Name

SkillFolio AI

AI-Powered Student Career Portfolio Platform

---

## Executive Summary

SkillFolio AI is an AI-powered platform that helps students create professional portfolios showcasing their projects, skills, certifications, and achievements.

Many talented students struggle to stand out during internships and placements because traditional resumes fail to represent their actual capabilities. SkillFolio AI aims to solve this problem by providing AI-generated recruiter-friendly portfolios.

## Key Findings

- Large target market of students and fresh graduates.
- Strong problem severity among students.
- Growing demand for portfolio-based hiring.
- MVP can be launched quickly using no-code tools.
- Further validation required before scaling.

## MVP Recommendation

Build First

Core Features

- AI Portfolio Generator
- Portfolio Templates
- AI Project Description Writer
- Multi-Discipline Support
- Public Portfolio Link
- Mobile Responsive Design
- Basic Recruiter Analytics

---

Do Not Build Initially

- Mobile Application
- ATS Integrations
- University Dashboard
- AI Career Coach
- Advanced Recruiter Marketplace

## Biggest Customer Insight

Many students possess valuable skills, projects, and certifications but fail to present them effectively through traditional resumes. Existing solutions are either too professional, too technical, or not designed specifically for students.

---

## Most Important MVP Feature

An AI-powered Portfolio Builder that automatically transforms projects, skills, certifications, and achievements into a recruiter-friendly portfolio within minutes.

---

## Key Learnings

- Customer understanding should come before product development.
- Solving a real problem is more important than adding more features.
- MVP should focus only on core value creation.
- Continuous validation reduces startup risk.
- Customer feedback is essential before scaling.

---

## Final Verdict

🟡 Promising but Unvalidated

SkillFolio AI addresses a genuine and recurring problem faced by students. The market opportunity is significant, but additional validation with students, recruiters, and institutions is required before full-scale development.
