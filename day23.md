## Day 23 - Customer & MVP Blueprint

## Startup Name

SkillFolio AI

## Executive Summary

SkillFolio AI is an AI-powered student career portfolio platform that helps students showcase projects, skills, certifications, 
and achievements beyond traditional resumes. The platform aims to improve internship and job opportunities by providing recruiter-friendly AI-generated portfolios.

---

Location| India

Willingness to Pay| ₹499–₹1499/year
