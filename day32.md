## Day 32 – Marketing Strategy Simulator

## Objective

Built and explored an AI-powered Marketing Strategy Simulator using Claude to understand how marketing strategists make decisions based on audience, platforms,
content strategy, and unexpected business situations.

## Task Completed

-Read the provided resources.
-Watched the solution video.
-Opened Claude and set the effort level to Low.
-Generated a complete HTML Marketing Strategy Simulator.
-Saved and executed the HTML application locally.
-Explored the simulator using Random Client mode.
-Analyzed the target audience and business challenge.
-Selected the most suitable marketing platforms.
-Chose three content pillars.
-Generated a 30-day marketing roadmap.
-Responded to a simulated marketing crisis.
-Reviewed the final Growth Report.
-Studied the reusable Claude prompts provided after each section.
-Captured screenshots of the complete workflow.

## Business Scenario

-Business: Boutique Fitness Studio (Reformer Pilates)
-Target Audience: Women aged 30–50 looking for low-impact, high-result fitness workouts near their neighborhood.
-Business Challenge: Strong customer retention but low local awareness.

## Selected Marketing Platforms

-LinkedIn
-YouTube
-Email Newsletter

## Selected Content Pillars

-Product Education
-Behind the Scenes
-Social Proof & Reviews

## Marketing Response

-When customer complaints started trending, I selected:
"Respond publicly, acknowledge the issue, and share a fix."
-This demonstrated transparency, accountability, and long-term customer trust.

## Key Learnings

-Marketing starts with understanding the audience, not creating content.
-Choosing fewer but relevant platforms is more effective than being everywhere.
-Content pillars help maintain consistency and build a strong brand identity.
-A structured 30-day roadmap ensures focused execution.
-Transparency during crises strengthens customer trust.
-AI can effectively assist in strategic marketing planning and decision-making.
