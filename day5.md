## Key Learnings

1. Context significantly improves AI outputs.
2. Detailed prompts generate more accurate responses.
3. AI performs better when goals and constraints are provided.
4. Context Engineering is important for real-world AI applications.

## Conclusion

Providing relevant context helps AI generate more useful, personalized, and actionable responses.
