# Day 57 - PrepAI Capstone | Product Refinement & User Experience

## Objective

The objective of Day 57 was to refine and improve the PrepAI Capstone Project after completing the MVP on Day 6.

The primary focus was on improving the overall product experience without changing the core vision or unnecessarily introducing new features.

The application was reviewed from three perspectives:

* Senior Product Designer
* UI/UX Designer
* Senior Software Engineer

The goal was to make PrepAI more polished, professional, responsive, accessible, and portfolio-ready.

---

## Project Overview

PrepAI is an AI-powered mock interview platform that helps users practice technical interviews in areas such as Java, Data Structures & Algorithms, and Web Development through an interactive interview experience.

### Tech Stack

**Backend:**

* Java Spring Boot

* Vite
