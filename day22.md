## Day 22 – Startup Validation Report

## Startup Idea

SkillFolio AI – An AI-powered student career portfolio platform that helps students showcase their projects, certifications, achievements, 
and skills in a professional way.

## Objective

The goal of this task was to validate a startup idea using AI-powered market research and startup validation frameworks.

## Key Findings

## Executive Summary

SkillFolio AI addresses a major problem faced by students who struggle to present their practical skills through traditional resumes.
The platform aims to bridge the gap between students and recruiters through AI-generated portfolios and career insights.

## Validation Score

- Problem Validation Score: 72/100
- Founder-Market Fit Score: 8.1/10
- Recommendation: Conditional Go ✅

## Problem Being Solved

Students often miss internships and job opportunities because resumes fail to effectively showcase projects, certifications, and practical skills.

## Target Audience

- College Students
- Fresh Graduates
- Bootcamp Learners
- Recruiters
- Universities and Training Institutes

## TAM / SAM / SOM Analysis

Metric| Value
TAM| $12 Billion
SAM| $1.2 Billion
SOM| $60 Million

## Competitor Analysis

Main competitors identified:

- LinkedIn
- GitHub
- Behance
- Internshala
- Unstop
- Peerlist

## Market Gap Analysis

The report identified a gap for an AI-native portfolio platform specifically designed for students across multiple disciplines.

## Risk Assessment

- LinkedIn may introduce similar features.
- Student willingness to pay is limited.
- Institutional sales cycles may be slow.
- Portfolio adoption in India is still developing.

## Go / No-Go Recommendation

Conditional Go.

The idea should first be validated with:

- 50+ student interviews
- 100+ waitlist signups
- 2–3 institutional pilot programs

before investing heavily in development.

## 30-Day Action Plan

Week 1:

- Conduct user interviews
- Analyze competitors
- Create landing page

Week 2:

- Build no-code MVP
- Recruit beta users
- Contact placement officers

Week 3:

- Test MVP
- Create marketing content
- Schedule institutional calls

Week 4:

- Analyze feedback
- Meet mentors/investors
- Make final build decision

## Key Learnings

- Learned how startup validation works.
- Understood Founder-Market Fit evaluation.
- Explored TAM, SAM, and SOM calculations.
- Studied competitors and market gaps.
- Learned how to assess startup risks.
- Created a structured action plan for idea validation.
