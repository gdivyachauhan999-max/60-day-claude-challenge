Day 8 - Personal Environmental Health Analyzer

 ## Overview

Today, I built a Personal Environmental Health Analyzer Dashboard using Claude AI. 
The goal was to analyze environmental health metrics such as Air Quality Index (AQI), PM2.5, PM10, water quality indicators, and city-wise environmental conditions through an interactive dashboard.

## Features Implemented

- Interactive AQI Comparison Dashboard
- PM2.5 and PM10 Analysis Charts
- AQI Category Distribution Visualization
- City Ranking (Cleanest to Most Polluted)
- Environmental Health Score Calculation
- City Detail Cards
- Environmental Health Analysis
- Personal Environmental Report Card
- Insights Panel
- Personalized Recommendations
- Interactive Filters
