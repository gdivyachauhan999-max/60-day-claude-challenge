## Day 29 – Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab

## Objective

Built and completed the Operation Lifeline: Supply Chain Crisis Lab simulation to understand how executive decisions, supplier relationships, crisis communication, 
and AI investments influence supply chain resilience.

---

## What I Did

- Generated the complete React HTML simulator using Claude.
- Ran the Supply Chain Crisis simulation.
- Reviewed the randomly generated company profile.
- Analyzed the assigned Port Strike crisis.
- Selected three crisis response actions.
- Completed all supplier negotiation rounds.
- Answered all CEO leadership scenarios.
- Selected two AI-powered supply chain investments.
- Reviewed the final executive dashboard and performance metrics.

---

## Company Profile

- Industry: Aerospace Components
- Revenue: $5.2 Billion
- Factories: 3
- Warehouses: 8
- Suppliers: 240
- Inventory: 90 Days
- Average Lead Time: 60 Days

---

## Crisis Scenario

A major Port Strike disrupted shipments and delayed critical inventory, impacting delivery speed, inventory availability, profitability, and customer satisfaction.

---

## Crisis Response Actions Selected

- Activate Emergency Suppliers
- Production Rerouting
- Customer Communication Blitz

---

## Supplier Negotiation Strategy

- Proposed a 12-month volume commitment.
- Negotiated a 20% premium with faster payment terms.
- Requested partial shipments every five days.
- Finalized a two-year preferred supplier agreement with KPIs and exit clauses.

---

## CEO Leadership Decisions

- Reduced inventory only for slow-moving SKUs.
- Strengthened supplier relationships through executive communication.
- Chose transparent crisis communication.
- Approved a complete supply chain audit.
- Balanced employee retention using a raise plus retention bonus.

---

## AI Investments

- Supplier Risk Monitor
- Demand Forecasting AI

---

## Final Performance

- Leadership: 91%
- Negotiation: 83%
- Customer Satisfaction: 88%
- Risk Management: 75%
- Resilience: 43%
- Cost Control: 28%

---

## Key Learnings

- Supply chain resilience must be built before a crisis occurs.
- Supplier relationships are strategic business assets.
- Transparent communication protects customer trust.
- AI enables proactive risk detection and better forecasting.
- Executive decisions require balancing operational continuity with financial performance.
