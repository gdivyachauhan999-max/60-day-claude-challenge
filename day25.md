## Day 25 – AI Shark Tank Simulator

## Objective
Built and tested an AI Shark Tank Simulator using Claude AI. Generated a complete HTML application, simulated a startup pitch,
answered investor questions, analyzed results, tested leaderboard functionality, and downloaded the pitch report.

## Startup Details

## Startup Name

InterviewAce AI

## Problem Statement
Millions of students and fresh graduates struggle to prepare for job interviews and often fail due to lack of personalized feedback, 
mock interviews, and industry-specific guidance.

## Solution
InterviewAce AI is an AI-powered interview coach that conducts realistic mock interviews, analyzes answers, provides instant feedback,
identifies weak areas, and generates personalized improvement plans to help candidates crack placements and job interviews.

## Revenue Model
Freemium model with premium plans at $10/month, corporate partnerships, and college placement training packages.

## Target Audience
College students, fresh graduates, job seekers, and professionals preparing for technical and HR interviews.

## Funding Ask
$50,000 for 10% equity.

## Investor Questions & Responses

Q1: What makes InterviewAce AI defensible against large competitors entering your space?
Answer: InterviewAce AI's competitive advantage lies in its proprietary interview intelligence engine. 
Every mock interview generates structured data on communication skills, technical knowledge, confidence, and problem-solving ability.
Over time, this creates a unique dataset that continuously improves our AI feedback accuracy. Additionally, partnerships with colleges,
training institutes, and recruiters create a strong distribution network.

Q2: Walk me through your unit economics — cost to acquire a customer vs lifetime value?
Answer: Our CAC is approximately $9 through university partnerships, referral programs, and digital marketing. 
The average premium user spends around $15/month and remains active for nearly 20 months. This results in an LTV of approximately $300 per customer,
giving an LTV:CAC ratio above 33:1.

## Learnings
-Investors focus heavily on traction, validation, and execution.
-Strong ideas alone are not enough; proof of market demand is important.
-Unit economics (CAC vs LTV) significantly influence funding decisions.
-Proprietary data and strategic partnerships improve startup defensibility.
-AI can rapidly generate and simulate startup pitch evaluations.
