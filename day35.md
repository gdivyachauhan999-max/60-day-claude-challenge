# Day 35 - Prompt Puzzle (Software Development)

## Objective
The objective of this task was to improve prompt engineering skills by identifying what makes an effective AI prompt.
The activity focused on writing clear, specific, and structured prompts while avoiding unnecessary instructions, filler words, and over-engineering.

## Key Learnings

- Clear and specific prompts generate higher-quality AI responses.
- Defining a role (e.g., Senior Engineer, QA Engineer, Mentor) improves output quality.
- Specifying the expected output format makes responses more consistent.
- Removing filler words improves prompt clarity and efficiency.
- Avoiding unnecessary instructions prevents over-engineering.
- Breaking complex tasks into structured instructions produces better results.
- Including constraints such as response length, categories, and formatting leads to more accurate outputs.

---

## Challenges Faced

- Identifying irrelevant prompt blocks during the Build the Prompt challenges.
- Distinguishing between concise prompts and over-detailed prompts.
- Learning to remove filler words without changing the intent of the prompt.

---

## Outcome

This activity strengthened my understanding of prompt engineering principles such as clarity, specificity, structured instructions, role assignment, and output constraints.
It also demonstrated how well-designed prompts significantly improve the quality and relevance of AI-generated responses.
