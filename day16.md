# Day 16 – Build Your First Stock Research Skill

## Objective
To create a reusable stock fundamental research skill using Claude and use it to analyze and compare stocks.

## Custom Skill Created
**Skill Name:** stock-fundamental-research

## Stock Analysis Performed
- Company: TCS
- Generated a detailed fundamental analysis report.

## Stock Comparison
Compared the following companies:
- TCS
- Infosys

## Key Insights
### TCS
- Reviewed the company's fundamentals.
- Explored valuation metrics.
- Analyzed ownership patterns.
- Identified potential risk factors.

### TCS vs Infosys
- Compared valuation and growth aspects.
- Evaluated strengths and weaknesses of both companies.
- Understood differences in business performance and investment considerations.

## Key Learnings
- Learned how to create a custom skill in Claude.
- Understood the importance of structured prompts.
- Explored how AI can assist in stock fundamental research.
- Learned to generate reusable reports without rewriting prompts.
- Practiced comparing companies using the same research framework.
