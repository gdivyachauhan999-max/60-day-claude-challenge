# Day 24 - Business Strategy Report

## Startup
SkillFolio AI

## Objective
Build a complete business strategy and sustainability roadmap for SkillFolio AI.

## Business Reality Check

SkillFolio AI solves a genuine problem where students struggle to showcase skills and projects effectively to recruiters.
Traditional resumes fail to communicate practical abilities, creating an opportunity for AI-powered portfolio solutions.

## Business Model Canvas

### Customer Segments
- Students
- Recruiters
- Educational Institutions

### Value Proposition
- AI-powered portfolio creation
- Better student visibility
- Faster recruiter discovery

### Channels
- LinkedIn
- Instagram
- Campus Ambassadors
- Placement Cells

### Revenue Streams
- Student Pro Subscription
- Recruiter Subscription
- Institutional Licensing

## Revenue & Pricing Strategy

### Free Plan
₹0

### Student Pro
₹499 – ₹1499/year

### Recruiter Plan
₹5,000 – ₹20,000/month

### Institution Plan
₹50,000 – ₹3,00,000/year

## Go-To-Market Strategy

- Campus communities
- Placement season campaigns
- Instagram content marketing
- LinkedIn thought leadership
- Placement officer outreach

## Customer Acquisition Strategy

- Campus ambassadors
- Referral programs
- Social media marketing
- Recruiter partnerships
- College collaborations

## First 100 Users Plan

1. Interview 50 students
2. Launch waitlist
3. Build no-code MVP
4. Recruit beta testers
5. Partner with placement cells

## Competitive Position & Moat

### Competitors
- LinkedIn
- Peerlist
- Unstop

### Moat
- Student-focused experience
- AI portfolio generation
- Recruiter-friendly presentation
- Institutional partnerships

## Reverse SWOT Analysis

### Strengths
- Strong problem statement
- Large target market

### Weaknesses
- Early-stage startup
- No technical co-founder

### Opportunities
- Growing portfolio-based hiring
- AI adoption in education

### Threats
- LinkedIn competition
- Slow institutional sales cycles

## Investor One-Liner

"SkillFolio AI helps students transform their skills, projects, and achievements into recruiter-ready AI portfolios."

## Founder Pitch

SkillFolio AI is building the future of student portfolios by enabling students to showcase their real capabilities beyond traditional resumes.

## Investment Scorecard

- Market Opportunity: Strong
- Problem Severity: High
- Business Model: Moderate
- Validation Status: Early

## Visual Dashboard Insights

### Revenue Streams
- Student Pro
- Recruiter Subscription
- Institution Licensing

### Key Growth Focus
- Student Acquisition
- Recruiter Validation
- Institutional Partnerships

## Top Risks

1. LinkedIn copies similar features.
2. Students resist paying.
3. No technical co-founder.
4. Slow B2B sales cycle.
5. Portfolio adoption challenges.

## Founder Action Sheet

1. Conduct 50 student interviews.
2. Build MVP.
3. Create landing page.
4. Recruit technical co-founder.
5. Contact placement officers.
6. Conduct usability testing.
7. Create marketing content.
8. Perform competitor analysis.
9. Interview recruiters.
10. Seek mentor feedback.

## One Metric That Matters (OMTM)

Portfolio Completion Rate > 60% in first 100 users. 2

## Key Learnings

- Validation is more important than scaling.
- Recruiter demand must be proven early.
- B2B revenue is likely stronger than student subscriptions.
- Partnerships can create long-term defensibility.
- Data and network effects can become future moats.

## Final Verdict

🟡 VALIDATE

SkillFolio AI has a real market opportunity, but the next 30 days should focus on validation, recruiter conversations, institutional interest,
and MVP testing before pursuing scale or funding. Overall Score: 66/100 (Conditional GO).
