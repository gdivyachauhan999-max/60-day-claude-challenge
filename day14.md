## Final Verdict

⚠️ Apply with Caution

This opportunity does not necessarily appear fraudulent; however, it presents several indicators of a startup environment with high expectations and potential work-life balance concerns. Candidates should seek clarity regarding compensation, workload, role scope, and remote work expectations before proceeding.

---

## Smart Interview Questions

1. The role is titled "Junior" but requires 3–5 years of experience. How are the compensation and responsibilities benchmarked for this position?

2. What does a typical workweek look like, and how often are employees expected to work weekends or after hours? How is this time compensated?

3. Considering the wide range of responsibilities listed, how are these tasks distributed within the team?

4. For employees located outside Bengaluru, how frequently is office travel required, and are travel expenses covered by the company?

5. Can you share insights into the engineering team's growth, retention, and organizational stability since the company's founding?

---

## Key Learnings

- AI can assist job seekers in evaluating opportunities beyond surface-level descriptions.
- Unrealistic expectations in job postings can signal workload and compensation concerns.
- Salary transparency is an important factor when assessing job quality.
- Startup environments may provide rapid learning opportunities but can also involve increased pressure and role ambiguity.
- Asking targeted interview questions helps candidates make informed career decisions.
