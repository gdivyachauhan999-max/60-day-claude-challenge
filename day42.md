# Day 42 – Personal Financial Command Center

## Overview

Today, I built a complete **Personal Financial Command Center** using Claude AI. The application acts as a personal finance dashboard that helps users track income, expenses,
savings, financial goals, investments, cash flow, and overall financial health through an interactive and responsive interface.

---

## Features

### Dashboard
- Financial Health Score
- Monthly Allowance Overview
- Monthly Spending Summary
- Remaining Savings
- Savings Rate
- Spending by Category Chart
- AI-Powered Recommendations

### Allowance & Income
- Add Monthly Income Source
- Income Summary
- Average Income Tracking
- Income Management

### Expense Tracker
- Add New Expenses
- Expense History
- Category-wise Expense Breakdown
- Food, Entertainment & Transport Tracking

### Budget Planner
- Needs vs Wants vs Savings Planning
- Budget vs Actual Comparison
- Budget Visualization Charts

### Goals & What-If Simulator
- Multiple Financial Goals
- Savings Progress
- Goal Timeline Estimation
- What-If Monthly Savings Simulator

### Investing 101
- Investment Basics
- Beginner Investment Checklist
- Compound Interest Calculator
- Investment Growth Graph

### Cash Flow
- Monthly Income vs Expense Comparison
- Cash Flow Visualization

### AI Insights
- Personalized Financial Suggestions
- Spending Analysis
- Savings Recommendations
- Goal Improvement Suggestions

### Learn & Resources
- Financial Literacy Checklist
- Budgeting Resources
- Investment Resources
- AI Learning Prompts

### Printable Report
- Financial Summary
- Monthly Income & Expenses
- Financial Health Score
- Expense Breakdown
- Goal Progress Report
- Print-Friendly Report
