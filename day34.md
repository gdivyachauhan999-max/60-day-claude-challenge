## Day 34 – Marketing Detective

## Objective

Built and explored an interactive Marketing Detective HTML application using Claude AI. Investigated a simulated marketing campaign, analyzed evidence,
identified the root cause of the campaign failure, and reviewed the expert learning report.

## What I Did

-Generated the complete HTML application using Claude.
-Saved and opened the HTML file in the browser.
-Selected a preferred color theme.
-Accepted a new marketing investigation.
-Analyzed campaign details and performance metrics.
-Organized evidence cards on the investigation board.
-Identified the primary marketing mistake.
-Reviewed the expert explanation and final verdict.
-Studied the Marketing Learning Report.
-Explored randomized marketing investigation cases.
-Captured screenshots of the completed investigation.

## Key Learnings

-Strong engagement does not always result in high conversions.
-A poorly designed checkout or landing page can significantly increase cart abandonment.
-Customer feedback is valuable for identifying real user pain points.
-Marketing performance should be evaluated using the complete conversion funnel, not only clicks or impressions.
-Evidence-based analysis leads to better marketing decisions.
-User experience plays a critical role in campaign success.

## Case Summary

 Campaign: Lumora Skincare

Main Problem Identified: The campaign generated good traffic and engagement, but the landing page had a confusing multi-step checkout process that caused high cart abandonment and
reduced conversions.

Supporting Evidence:

-High CTR but low conversions.
-Customer comments highlighted checkout confusion.
-Creative performance was strong, indicating the issue occurred after users clicked the advertisement.

## Outcome
Successfully completed the Marketing Detective investigation, identified the root cause of the campaign failure, and gained practical insights into conversion funnel analysis and
marketing optimization.
