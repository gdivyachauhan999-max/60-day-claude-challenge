## Objective
Built and tested an AI-powered Supply Chain Control Tower simulation using Claude AI. The objective was to monitor operational alerts, prioritize incidents,
make corrective decisions, and maintain key supply chain KPIs throughout the simulation.

---


## Simulation Summary
The simulation focused on handling real-time operational disruptions across a global supply chain network. Multiple alerts were generated during the shift, 
requiring quick decision-making to minimize business impact.

### Responsibilities
- Monitored incoming operational alerts.
- Prioritized incidents based on urgency.
- Selected corrective actions for each alert.
- Observed KPI changes after every decision.
- Managed multiple operational disruptions simultaneously.
- Completed the simulation successfully.
