# 🎉 Day 60 - PrepAI Capstone | Graduation & Version 1.0.0 Release

## Objective

Day 60 marked the successful completion of both the **AB Talks 60-Day Claude AI Challenge** and the **10-Day PrepAI Capstone Sprint**.

The objective was to perform the final project review, prepare professional portfolio assets, finalize documentation, release **PrepAI v1.0.0**, and reflect on the complete development journey from idea to a production-ready application.

---

# Project Overview

**PrepAI** is an AI-powered mock interview platform designed to help learners prepare for technical interviews through an interactive and structured experience.

The project was developed as the capstone of the AB Talks 60-Day Claude AI Challenge, following a complete Software Development Lifecycle—from planning and development to testing, deployment, documentation, and final release.
