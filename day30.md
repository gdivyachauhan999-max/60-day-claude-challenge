## Day 30 – Supply Chain Builder (Claude AI)

## 📌 Objective

Built and explored an interactive Supply Chain Builder simulator using Claude AI to understand how different supply chain decisions affect business performance across cost,
delivery speed, resilience, customer satisfaction, and sustainability.

## 🛠️ Tools Used

-HTML
-CSS
-JavaScript
-Google Chrome
-Git & GitHub

-Industry: Toys & Games
-Markets: India, UAE, Saudi Arabia
-Demand Pattern: Seasonal
-Product: Plush Toys

1. Supplier Strategy
✅ Single Supplier

## ⭐ Overall Supply Chain Score
60 / 100
   
## ✅ Strengths
-Strong Cost Efficiency.
-Good Sustainability performance.
-Balanced transportation and inventory planning.
-Domestic manufacturing improved delivery speed.
-Regional warehouses enhanced customer reach.

## ⚠️ Weaknesses
-Low resilience due to dependency on a single supplier.
-Customer satisfaction can still be improved.
-Supply chain remains vulnerable to supplier disruptions.

## 🚨 Biggest Risk
The business depends on a single supplier. If that supplier experiences disruptions such as production delays, quality issues, or natural disasters,
the entire supply chain may be affected.

## 💡 Recommended Improvements
-Add backup suppliers to reduce supply chain risk.
-Increase safety stock during seasonal demand periods.
-Continuously monitor demand trends and adjust inventory accordingly.

## 📚 Key Learnings
-Every supply chain decision involves trade-offs.
-Lower costs can sometimes increase operational risk.
-Domestic manufacturing improves responsiveness and delivery speed.
-Regional warehouses improve customer satisfaction and accessibility.
-Inventory planning plays a major role during seasonal demand.
-Diversifying suppliers increases overall resilience.
-Data-driven decision-making leads to a stronger and more balanced supply chain.

## 🎯 Outcome
Successfully built and tested an AI-generated Supply Chain Builder simulator using Claude AI, explored different optimization strategies, analyzed business trade-offs, 
and documented the complete learning process in GitHub.
